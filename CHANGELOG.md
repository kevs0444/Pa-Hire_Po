# Pa-Hire Po Updates

See what is new in your job search and resume workspace.

## November 16, 2026

### Real-Time Activity Bell and Loading Feedback
- **Live Notification Bell:** A new Bell icon in the top bar tracks saved jobs, applications, tailored resumes, and job scan results in real time across open tabs.
- **Database Loading Indicators:** Clear loading indicators and subtle confirmation animations now show when your workspace loads or saves updates to your account.
- **What is New Changelog:** Open What is New anytime from the notification panel to catch up on recent feature updates.

### Applied Companies and Resume Pop-Out Preview
- **Applied and Resumes Tab:** Review every company you applied to in one organized workspace, update your stage (Applied, Interviewing, Offer Received, or Closed), and save personal notes.
- **Pop-Out Submitted Resume:** Click Show Resume on any application or resume version to open only the exact resume you sent, ready to print or edit again.
- **Saved and Hidden Job Lists:** Bookmark jobs to revisit later or hide roles you do not want to see. Your saved, applied, and hidden lists stay stored on your account.

### Global Job Role Library, Settings Sidepanel, and Desktop App
- **Right-Side Settings Panel:** Edit your personal contact details, target salary range, work setup, and preferred job roles from one slide-over panel.
- **Searchable Job Role Library:** Search beyond data and tech roles to pick and prioritize your top 5 to 10 target roles across any career field.
- **Desktop App Setup Guide:** Install Pa-Hire Po on your desktop directly from the Settings panel for quick access from your taskbar.
