# Session Log — 2026-11-20 (Multi-Platform Extraction, Title+Company Search, Internet Fallback, Copy URL & Job Tracking)

## Focus Goal
Ship `v1.0.5` release across two sequential tasks:
1. **Task 1 — Dynamic 3-Platform Job Extraction, Specific Title + Company Search, Internet Fallback & `Copy URL`**:
   - Added dedicated **Job title or keyword** + **Company name (optional)** search inputs (plus `"Role at Company"` smart parsing) without overwriting `activeProfile.primaryRole` unless the user clicks **Save as Target Role**.
   - Expanded LinkedIn (multi-page Guest API + top-8 description enrichment + `ts-jobspy`), JobStreet (v5 `pageSize=24` + HTML/JSON-LD fallback), and Indeed (`ts-jobspy` + HTML mosaic fallback), backed by a zero-key site-scoped Internet Web Search fallback (`src/server/scrapers/web-fallback.ts`) using DuckDuckGo HTML, Yahoo Search HTML, and `ts-jobspy` Google Jobs.
   - Added strict search correlation (`filterBySearchCorrelation`), replaced stale unsaved suggestions on explicit search while preserving `Saved`, `Applied`, `Hidden`, and `Queued` jobs, and placed `CopyUrlButton.tsx` across Job Cards, `JobDetailPane.tsx`, and `TopChoicesSpotlight.tsx`.
2. **Task 2 — Rename `Applied & Resumes` → `Job Tracking` (`Resume Applied`, Rich `+ Add Job Application` & `Copy URL`)**:
   - Renamed the tab across `AppTopNavbar.tsx`, `LandingPageView.tsx`, `HowItWorksModal.tsx`, and `ApplicationsCompilationView.tsx` to **Job Tracking** with sub-features **Applied Jobs**, **Resume Applied** (filterable by `All`, `✓ Applied`, and `Saved`), **Saved Jobs**, and **Hidden Jobs**.
   - Upgraded **`+ Add Job Application`** with Company/Job URL (**Paste URL** auto-detection of platform, company, and role + **Copy URL**), Company Name, Job Title, Platform, Location, Work Setup, Salary/Offer, Stage, Date Applied, Resume Applied picker, and Notes, plus inline detail editing and 1-click `CopyUrlButton` across tracked and saved listings.
