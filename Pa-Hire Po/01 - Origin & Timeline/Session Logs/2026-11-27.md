# Session Log — 2026-11-27 ("New to me" Job Search Filter & Cross-Board Applied Deduplication)

## Summary
Designed (via `/plan` + `/grill-me`), implemented, and released **`v1.0.10`**: a 1-click **"New to me"** job search toggle pill modeled after SEEK/JobStreet's filter placement, hiding jobs already acted upon (`applied`, `saved`, `hidden`, `queued`) as well as cross-board duplicate listings matching a role the user already applied to.

## Key Changes
1. **Profile Preference (`src/types/profile.ts`)**:
   - Added `newToMeOnly?: boolean` to `UserProfile`, defaulting to `true` (`ON` by default) and syncing automatically via `user_profiles.profile_data` JSONB and local storage.
2. **Filter & Duplicate Matching Logic (`src/features/jobs/lib/job-filters.ts`)**:
   - Implemented `normalizeCompanyTitleKey`, `buildAppliedRoleKeys`, and `isJobNewToMe` to keep only `suggested` jobs not in `queuedJobIds` and not duplicating normalized `company::title` keys of already `applied` jobs (retaining merely viewed listings in `viewedJobIds`).
3. **Banner Toggle Pill & Empty-State Recovery (`JobSearchBanner.tsx`, `JobDiscoveryFeed.tsx`, `PleaseHireMeDashboard.tsx`)**:
   - Placed the **`New to me`** toggle pill (`aria-pressed`, emerald indicator dot + `Check` icon, `civic-touch-target`) at the front of Row 2's filter bar ahead of `Pay`.
   - Bypassed `New to me` on explicit `Saved`, `Applied`, and `Hidden` `/jobs` tabs, routed Home **Saved jobs** sidebar clicks directly to `applied` or `saved`, and added a 1-click **Show applied & saved jobs** button when `New to me` hides all loaded listings.

## Verification
- Pre-push: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` (`24/24` routes) all exited `0`.
- Production rollout: Pending push & live verification.
