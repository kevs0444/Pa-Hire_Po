# Session Log — 2026-11-24 (JobStreet-Inspired Minimal UI, Mobile Bottom Bar, 6 Pill Filter Popovers, Recent Searches, Activity Workspace & Auto-Saved Applied Jobs)

## Focus Goal
Adapt the clean, airy JobStreet experience across **Header**, **Account Popover**, **Search Banner & Pill Popovers**, **Home Discovery Feed**, and **Activity (`Saved` / `Applied` / `Resume Applied` / `Hidden`)** while making the entire workflow mobile-first and responsive (`320px` to desktop), preserving **Pa-Hire Po's Civic Career Utility** palette, and syncing new state in Supabase JSONB.

## Key Changes Implemented
1. **Viewport, Safe Areas, Touch Targets & `@container` CSS (`layout.tsx`, `globals.css`):**
   - Enabled `width=device-width`, `initialScale=1`, and `viewportFit=cover` in `layout.tsx`.
   - Added `scroll-padding-top` and mobile `scroll-padding-bottom` on `html`, `text-wrap: balance`/`pretty`, `.discovery-card-container` (`container: job-card / inline-size`), `.civic-touch-target` (`44×44px` under `@media (pointer: coarse)`), and `100dvh`/`100dvw` popover constraints gated by `@supports (position-area: block-end)`.
2. **Minimal 4-Tab Header, Fixed Mobile Bottom Bar & Account Popover (`AppTopNavbar.tsx`, `UserAvatarMenu.tsx`):**
   - Desktop keeps 4 crisp underline tabs (**Job Search**, **Resume Studio AI**, **Activity**, **Analytics**) with `aria-current="page"`.
   - Mobile (`< 768px`) keeps `<header>` to 1 compact row (`~56px`) and adds a fixed thumb-friendly 4-tab Bottom Navigation Bar with safe-area padding (`Jobs`, `Resume AI`, `Activity`, `Analytics`).
   - `UserAvatarMenu.tsx` uses native `popover="auto"` (no invalid `role="menu"`/`aria-haspopup`), `44px` coarse-pointer rows, and JS rect clamping fallback.
3. **Full-Width Civic Search Banner with 6 Pill Popovers & `Recent searches` (`JobSearchBanner.tsx`, `job-filters.ts`, `job-date-utils.ts`):**
   - Replaced the bulky filter panel with 6 native `popover="auto"` pills (**Pay** with `Annually`/`Monthly`/`Hourly` tabs & localized currency steps, **Type**, **Remote**, **Classification**, **Listing time** radio options `Any time`–`Last 30 days`, and **Boards & More**), `16px` mobile inputs (`text-base sm:text-sm`) to stop Safari auto-zoom, and a collapsible **Recent searches** card strip (`bg-[#062d57]` Light / `bg-[#141417]` Dark; desktop expanded with `Show less`, mobile collapsed by default).
4. **Home Discovery Feed, Mobile Switcher, Auto-Saved Applied Jobs & Focused Mobile Explorer (`JobDiscoveryFeed.tsx`, `DiscoveryJobCard.tsx`, `JobDetailPane.tsx`, `PleaseHireMeDashboard.tsx`):**
   - Added a `lg:hidden` 2-tab mobile switcher (`Recommended (N)` | `Saved jobs (N)`), `@container` cards with fluid `clamp()` titles, and focused `< 1024px` Explorer detail switching (`mobileDetailOpen` + `← Back to results`).
   - Auto-saves every applied job (`status === 'applied'`) into **Saved jobs** (`isJobSaved`, individually removable via `unsavedAppliedJobIds` without removing Applied status).
   - Synced `savedSearches` (cap 20), `recentSearches` (cap 8), `viewedJobIds` (cap 150), and `unsavedAppliedJobIds` (cap 200) inside Supabase `user_profiles.profile_data` JSONB (`free-tier-limits.ts`).
5. **Single-Column `Activity` Workspace with Full-Detail Editing & Clamped Notes (`ApplicationsCompilationView.tsx`, `PleaseHireMeDashboard.tsx`):**
   - Redesigned `/applications` into a centered single-column **Activity** workspace (**Saved**, **Applied**, **Resume Applied**, **Hidden**) with `⋮` `popover="auto"` card menus, **Show Resume** pop-out modals, `line-clamp-2` + **Show more / Show less** Notes previews, and a shared full-detail inline editor (**Job Title**, **Company**, **Platform**, **Stage**, **Date Applied**, **Location**, **Work Setup**, **Salary**, **Resume Version**, **URL**, and resizable multi-line **Notes** textarea) wired through `handleUpdateJobApplication`.

6. **Unified Expandable Activity & Versions Cards + `Resume Applied` Inline Editor (`ApplicationsCompilationView.tsx`, `ResumeVersionsDrawer.tsx`):**
   - Fixed unclamped multi-paragraph notes on **Resume Applied** cards and unified all **Activity** (`Saved`, `Applied`, `Resume Applied`, `Hidden`) and **Resume Versions Drawer** cards with a compact 2-line preview by default and a consistent **`Expand` / `Collapse`** button (`ChevronDown` / `ChevronUp`, `aria-expanded`) revealing full scrollable notes, descriptions, and format metadata.
   - Added inline **`Edit Details`** on **Resume Applied** cards (`Version Name`, `Company`, `Job Title`, `Applied / Sent` toggle, and multi-line `Notes`) backed by `onUpdateResumeRecord` (`PATCH /api/resumes/history`).
7. **Minimalist Tabbed Executive Analytics Dashboard — Direction A (`JobAnalyticsView.tsx`, `AutoApplyBlueprintPanel.tsx`, `globals.css`):**
   - Redesigned `/analytics` into 3 accessible tabs (**Overview** | **Skill Gaps** | **Apply Queue**) with `role="tablist"` keyboard navigation, `hidden="until-found"` + `beforematch` Ctrl+F reveal, a unified 4-KPI top ribbon, CSS `subgrid`-aligned board performance cards, unwrapped **Role Demand** rows (`Show all`), horizontal **Application Funnel** & segmented **Work Setup Split** bars, Missing vs. Matched **Skill Gaps** filtering, and native `<details>` collapsible **Saved Screening Answers**.
