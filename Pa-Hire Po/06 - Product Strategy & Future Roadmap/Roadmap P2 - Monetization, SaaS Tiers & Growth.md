# Roadmap P2 — Monetization, SaaS Tiers & Growth

> **Priority Note:** Deferred (`p2-later` / `backlog`) until public launch and core utility retention are proven.

## Strategic Why
Sustainably cover Vercel, Supabase Pro, and hosted Gemini API costs if active user count grows beyond free-tier thresholds (`FREE_TIER_LIMITS`).

## Scope Ideas (When Ready)
- **Free Tier (Generous Core):** Multi-board search, honest match scoring, local/BYOK tailoring, saved & applied tracking.
- **Pro Tier (Hosted Convenience):** Higher hosted AI tailoring quota, deeper resume snapshot history, automated daily role digest, and priority multi-region scans.
- **Billing Options:** Stripe (Global) / PayMongo or GCash-compatible checkout (Philippines).
